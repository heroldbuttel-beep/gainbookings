Questions, answered

Before we build.

Clear expectations from the first conversation.

How long does a booking system take?

The timeline depends on the number of experiences, current website, booking platform, and automation needs. You receive a clear schedule with your proposal before work begins.

Can you connect my current booking platform?

In many cases, yes. We review your booking provider during the free audit and confirm the best connection before recommending a solution.

What does Focier AI actually do?

Focier AI by GainBookings can answer approved traveler questions, recommend experiences, capture qualified leads, send booking links, follow up on incomplete reservations, and hand complex requests to your team.

Does the AI replace my team?

No. It handles repetitive questions and follow-up while keeping a human handoff available for special requests or decisions that need your team.

Will I own the website and customer relationship?

Your domain and business accounts stay in your name. Website ownership, connected tools, and responsibilities are documented clearly in the project agreement.

How much does it cost?

The website is scoped as a project. Focier AI uses a custom setup plus monthly management. After the free audit, you receive a clear proposal before committing.
